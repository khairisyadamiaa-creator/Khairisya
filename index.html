What is a Saccheri Quadrilateral?

A Saccheri quadrilateral has two equal legs perpendicular to the base. The two opposite summit angles are equal.

ABBase
AD = BCEqual legs
∠A = ∠B = 90°Base angles
∠C = ∠D = αSummit angles

Mathematical reasoning

90° + 90° + α + α < 360°
180° + 2α < 360°
α < 90°

A hyperbolic quadrilateral has an angle sum less than 360°. Therefore, the equal summit angles of a Saccheri quadrilateral are acute.
